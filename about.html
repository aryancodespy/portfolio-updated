My Journey

My journey didn’t start in marketing — it started with code.

In 2017, I began learning HTML, building simple websites out of curiosity. That quickly expanded into CSS and JavaScript, and later into Python and Kotlin. I was always interested in how things worked — not just how they looked.

But while building websites, I realized something important: a website without traffic is invisible.

That realization pulled me into digital marketing. I started with SEO, keyword research, and content strategy — learning how people search, what drives attention, and how organic growth actually works.

During this phase, I explored affiliate marketing, CPA, and PPC models. One of my early wins was generating around BDT 26,000 through organic SEO by applying keyword research strategically.

Along the way, I developed hands-on experience with tools like Google Search Console, Google Analytics, SEMrush, and Ahrefs — building a strong foundation in data-driven marketing.

In 2023, I stepped into the corporate world as a Digital Marketing Specialist. That’s where everything changed. I moved from experimentation to execution at scale — running Meta Ads, Google Ads, and full-funnel campaigns with real budgets and performance expectations.

Today, I operate at the intersection of marketing, technology, and growth systems.

I don’t just run campaigns — I build systems that connect everything: from traffic acquisition to conversion, from creative to analytics, from funnel to CRM.

This approach has allowed me to contribute to generating 40,000+ qualified leads and lead marketing efforts across multiple markets.

I believe the future belongs to people who can both think and build — and that’s exactly what I’m focused on.
